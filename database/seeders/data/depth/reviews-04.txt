<h2>Specifications</h2>
[[specs:redmi-a7-pro]]
<h2>The biggest screen for the least money</h2>
<p>At 6.9 inches, the Redmi A7 Pro has a display as large as phones costing ten times as much. That is the whole idea. Text can be enlarged without every message spilling off the page, YouTube is comfortable from arm's length, and video calls show faces at a decent size. For older eyes this is a better feature than any processor.</p>
<p>It also makes the phone wide. Someone with small hands will need both of them to type, and it will stick out of a trouser pocket. Try it in person if the owner is used to a compact phone.</p>
<p>The resolution is HD+, spread over all those inches, so letters look soft when you hold it close. At normal viewing distance with large text, it is acceptable. The 120Hz setting gives smoother movement, though the chip often cannot deliver 120 frames, and locking it at 60Hz saves power with little loss.</p>
<h2>Everyday speed</h2>
<p>The Unisoc T7250 and 4GB of memory handle one thing at a time. WhatsApp, calls, a wallet payment, a YouTube video: each works. Doing several together shows the limits. The phone takes a moment to open the camera, and returning to an app often means waiting for it to load again. It is usable with patience. It is not suitable for someone who is used to a mid-range phone.</p>
<h2>Storage is the choice that matters</h2>
<p>Pay the additional Rs 2,500 for 128GB. The cheaper version's 64GB will fill with system files, apps and WhatsApp media, and a full phone becomes slow. A memory card, if your unit has a free slot, can hold photos and music.</p>
<h2>Battery and charging</h2>
<p>The 6,000mAh battery runs for a day and a half of typical use, with two days possible if the phone is mostly for calls. Charging is 15W. Leave it plugged in overnight. The charger may or may not be in the box depending on the batch, so ask.</p>
<h2>Camera</h2>
<p>A single 13MP camera is honest in one respect: there is no fake second lens. In daylight it records what you point it at. Indoors and at night it is poor. The 8MP front camera is actually better suited to its job, which is video calls.</p>
<h2>Software and safety</h2>
<p>HyperOS 3 on Android 15 looks like other Redmi phones, so anyone who has used one will feel at home. Remove the extra apps and turn off recommendations. Update support on the A series is short compared with Samsung, so this is a phone for two to three years and not for five.</p>
<p>If you are giving it to a first-time user, set up a screen lock and Find My Device, disable unknown app sources, and install the official eSewa, Khalti or banking apps yourself from the Play Store. Fake wallet apps circulate in chat groups.</p>
<h2>What else is near Rs 22,000?</h2>
[[rivals:redmi-a7-pro]]
<p>The <a href="/products/realme-note-80">Realme Note 80</a> uses the same chip, has a slightly larger battery and a tougher body, and comes with 64GB only. The <a href="/products/infinix-smart-20">Infinix Smart 20</a> and <a href="/products/tecno-spark-go-3">Tecno Spark Go 3</a> compete closely. All of them are slow in a similar way, so choose by storage, service and screen.</p>
<p>The real alternative costs about Rs 4,000 more. The <a href="/products/samsung-galaxy-a07">Galaxy A07</a> with its Helio G99 feels like a different class, and it receives updates for much longer. If there is any way to stretch, stretch.</p>
<h2>Four situations where it makes sense</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>A grandparent</strong> who wants to see family on a large screen.</li>
<li><strong>A shop counter</strong> that needs a phone for QR payments, calls and a customer WhatsApp number.</li>
<li><strong>A driver</strong> who uses it as a map and call phone on a mount.</li>
<li><strong>A child's first phone</strong> with parental controls, where loss or breakage is likely.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Four situations where it does not</h2>
<ul>
<li>Online classes with many tabs and documents.</li>
<li>Games beyond Ludo, carrom and simple puzzles.</li>
<li>Taking photos at weddings or on trips.</li>
<li>Replacing a three-year-old mid-range phone. You would be going backwards.</li>
</ul>
<h2>New or used?</h2>
<p>For Rs 22,000 to 24,000, the used market offers phones that were far better when new: a Galaxy A52 or A53, a Redmi Note 11 Pro, a Poco X4. They have AMOLED screens and quicker chips. Against that, you get a worn battery, no warranty and a shorter future for updates. For a confident buyer who can check the phone and the MDMS registration, used can be the better deal. For a parent or a first-timer, new with a warranty is the safer choice.</p>
<h2>Making it last</h2>
<p>Use a case with raised corners, since a 6.9-inch screen is a large target. Keep 20GB of storage free. Update apps on Wi-Fi. Restart it every few days. And when the battery begins to fade after a couple of years, a replacement is inexpensive because Redmi parts are everywhere.</p>
<h2>A note on the price of cheap phones</h2>
<p>Memory has become more expensive through 2026, and entry-level phones feel it most, as our article on <a href="/news/electronic-price-hikes-nepal-ram-ssd-smartphones-analysis">memory prices</a> explains. Two years ago Rs 22,000 bought 6GB and a faster chip. Today it buys 4GB. That is not the fault of this phone, but it does mean that waiting for a sale, or spending a little more once, pays off better than usual.</p>
<h2>Verdict</h2>
<p>The Redmi A7 Pro does one thing well, which is to put a very large screen and a long battery into ordinary hands for little money. Buy the 128GB version for someone whose phone life is calls, chats, payments and video. For everybody else it is a false economy.</p>

<p>Realme has noticed that many buyers at this price break their phones before they outgrow them. The Note 80 answers with a body tested to a military standard for shock and temperature. <p>Expect a day and a half to two days. The 15W charging is slow, as it is on every phone at this level. Reverse charging at 6W is slow too, but it can bring a dead feature phone or a pair of earbuds back to life, and that has value on a long day out.</p>
<h2>A correction about the camera</h2>
<p>Specifications for this phone differ between sources. Our own specification sheet lists a 50MP main camera, while some listings, including the summary above, mention 8MP. Ask the shop to open the camera settings and check the maximum photo resolution before you buy. Either way, expect modest results: usable pictures in daylight and weak ones after sunset. The 5MP front camera is for calls.</p>

A memory card can take photos and videos, so buy one on the first day and set the camera to save to it. WhatsApp will still use the internal storage.</p>
<h2>Performance and screen</h2>

<h2>Who should consider it</h2>

<li><strong>Trekking porters and drivers</strong> who want a long battery.</li>

<li><strong>Parents of a teenager</strong> who has already broken two phones.</li>

<p>There is always a phone in the catalogue that does everything people actually complain about: battery, breakage, sunlight, rain. This year it is the Redmi Note 17 Pro. It will not win arguments about processors or cameras, and its owners will be too busy not charging it to care.</p>
