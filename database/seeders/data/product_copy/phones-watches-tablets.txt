=== blackview-zeno-2
<p>The Zeno 2 launched in October 2026 with Android 16 and an IP54 rating, unusual for a tablet. It is a good detail for kitchens, workshops and small children with juice. It costs Rs 19,999.</p>
<p>The processor is, unfortunately, the same slow Unisoc T310 found in tablets Rs 3,000 cheaper, and the 8.68-inch screen is 60Hz. You get 4GB and 128GB of slow eMMC storage.</p>
<p>We prefer the older <a href="/products/blackview-zeno-1">Zeno 1</a> with a better chip, a 90Hz display and more storage for Rs 17,999. Buy the Zeno 2 only if splash protection or the newest Android is important to you.</p>

=== blackview-zeno-5
<p>For Rs 29,999 the Zeno 5 gives you an 11-inch 90Hz display, 8GB and 128GB, Android 16 and IP54 protection. The Unisoc T7250 is an entry chip, adequate for study apps, video and documents.</p>
<p>Its battery is 8,300mAh with 18W charging. It weighs 573 grams.</p>
<p>The comparison to make is the <a href="/products/infinix-xpad-20-pro">Infinix XPAD 20 Pro</a>. For Rs 2,000 more you get a 12-inch screen, a faster Helio G100, 256GB and 45W charging, in a thinner aluminium body. The Zeno counters with newer Android and splash resistance. Most students should choose the Infinix.</p>

=== infinix-xpad-20-pro
<p>The XPAD 20 Pro is the affordable large tablet we suggest most often. It has a 12-inch 90Hz screen, aluminium body only 6.6mm thick, a Helio G100 Ultimate, and 45W charging. With 4GB and 128GB it is Rs 26,999. With 8GB and 256GB, Rs 31,999.</p>
<p>Pay for the 8GB version if you will use two apps together, for example a video lecture and notes, because that is what a 12-inch tablet is for. At 450 nits the panel is usable beside a window.</p>
<p>Wi-Fi is the older version 5, and Infinix does not promise many Android updates. Gaming is modest. Against the <a href="/products/redmi-pad-2">Redmi Pad 2</a>, the Infinix is larger and charges faster, while Xiaomi supports its tablets for longer.</p>

=== oneplus-pad-go
<p>The OnePlus Pad Go from late 2023 stands out for its shape. An 11.35-inch screen in 7:5 proportions shows more of a page than a wide tablet, so PDFs and web pages need less scrolling. It runs at 90Hz.</p>
<p>A Helio G99 with 8GB handles study and films with ease. The 8,000mAh battery charges at 33W. It began on Android 13, so its update life is mostly over.</p>
<p>At Rs 39,999 it is too expensive now. The <a href="/products/oneplus-pad-go-2">OnePlus Pad Go 2</a> is the newer model, and the <a href="/products/xiaomi-pad-7">Xiaomi Pad 7</a> has a far stronger chip. Consider it only used, or at a heavy discount.</p>
