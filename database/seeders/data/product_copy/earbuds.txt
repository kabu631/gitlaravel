=== oppo-enco-buds-3-pro
<p>Twelve hours per charge and 54 in all for Rs 3,499: the Enco Buds 3 Pro is one of the longest-lasting pairs at this price. Ten minutes gives four hours. They support two devices and have an IP55 rating, better than almost all buds near Rs 3,500.</p>
<p>The 12.4mm drivers are tuned warm. OPPO and OnePlus phone owners get settings inside the phone menu, while others use the HeyMelody app.</p>
<p>What is missing is noise cancelling. On that point the <a href="/products/boat-airdopes-161-anc-elite">boAt 161 ANC Elite</a> wins for less. For long calls, cycling and dusty roads, where you want to hear traffic anyway, the OPPO is the better tool.</p>

=== realme-buds-t100
<p>The Buds T100 came to Nepal in 2023 and still sells for Rs 2,499 because it is simple and dependable. Six hours per charge, 28 in total, 4.1 grams each and IPX5. Our record lists multipoint, which is worth testing in the shop as it is unusual for this model.</p>
<p>Ten-millimetre drivers are tuned for bass, and realme's Link app has three sound presets and a game mode.</p>
<p>It has aged in two ways: total playing time is short beside the newest pairs, and there is no noise cancelling. The <a href="/products/boat-airdopes-131-gen-2">boAt 131 Gen 2</a> costs the same and charges faster. The <a href="/products/redmi-buds-6-play">Redmi Buds 6 Play</a> lasts longer for equal money.</p>

=== realme-buds-t500-pro
<p>The T500 Pro brings flagship-style numbers to Rs 5,500: noise cancelling rated to 50dB, 13.5 hours per charge, 56 in total, Bluetooth 6.1 and LHDC 5.0 for high-resolution sound on supporting phones. Ten minutes of charging gives ten hours.</p>
<p>LHDC works with realme, OPPO, OnePlus, Xiaomi and some others. Samsung phones and iPhones fall back on AAC. Everything else works with any phone.</p>
<p>The buds are 4.5 grams with IP55, and multipoint is here. The nearest rival is the <a href="/products/cmf-buds-2a">CMF Buds 2a</a> at Rs 5,999, with a nicer app and less than two thirds of the battery life. On value, the realme is ahead. Listen for how its cancelling handles voices before deciding.</p>

=== remax-cozybuds-w33
<p>Remax is best known for cables and power banks, and the CozyBuds W33 is its everyday pair of earbuds at Rs 3,590. The details published are few: Bluetooth 5.4 and 13mm drivers. We could not confirm battery life, water protection or supported codecs.</p>
<p>That makes it hard to recommend over products with a full specification, such as the <a href="/products/oppo-enco-buds-3-pro">OPPO Enco Buds 3 Pro</a> at Rs 3,499 with 12 hours and IP55.</p>
<p>If you are drawn to the design, ask for the box and read the battery hours and the IP code. Also ask about the replacement period for a dead bud, since that is the most common failure in cheap pairs.</p>

=== remax-gamebuds-g7
<p>The GameBuds G7 is the gaming version of Remax's earbuds, with 13mm drivers and Bluetooth 5.4 in a 52 gram case. It costs Rs 3,640.</p>
<p>A game mode lowers audio delay, though we have no measured figure for it, and Remax does not list the codec. The sensible way to judge is simple. Pair it with your own phone, open your game, and tap the screen while listening. If the sound follows your finger closely enough for you, it passes.</p>
<p>Otherwise the <a href="/products/boat-immortal-131">boAt Immortal 131</a> covers the same ground for Rs 840 less, with published battery life.</p>

=== sony-wfc700n
<p>The WF-C700N was Sony's affordable noise-cancelling pair in 2023. Its strength is comfort. At 4.6 grams with a rounded shape, it fits small ears that reject larger buds, and the sound has Sony's natural balance even from a 5mm driver.</p>
<p>The battery is the weak part: seven and a half hours, and the case adds only one more charge, for about ten hours per the listing. Protection is IPX4. It connects to two devices.</p>
<p>At Rs 28,500 in Nepal it is badly placed. Sony has moved on, and its better <a href="/products/sony-wf-1000xm5">WF-1000XM5</a> is in another class. Newer brands give stronger cancelling for a quarter of the money. Wait for a clearance price near Rs 12,000.</p>

=== ultima-atom-buds-2
<p>The Ultima Atom Buds 2 at Rs 1,949 has a feature you do not expect at the price: multipoint, so your phone and laptop stay connected together. That is normally absent below Rs 2,500.</p>
<p>They use Bluetooth 5.4 and 10mm drivers with AAC, and carry an IPX4 rating. An hour fills the buds and two hours the case. No noise cancelling is listed.</p>
<p>Keep the invoice for the warranty. Compared with <a href="/products/kick-phantom-buds-x-elite">Kick's X Elite</a> at about the same price, choose Ultima for two devices and Kick for noise cancelling.</p>

=== redmi-buds-6-play
<p>A pair of 3.6 gram buds that plays for seven and a half hours, 36 with the case, and costs Rs 2,499. The Redmi Buds 6 Play is light enough to forget, and ten minutes of charging gives three hours.</p>
<p>Xiaomi lists Bluetooth 5.4 and IPX4. The sound is soft and bassy, good enough for music while walking. Xiaomi phones show a pop-up to connect; with other phones you pair it the ordinary way.</p>
<p>There is no noise cancelling and no multipoint. The <a href="/products/boat-airdopes-212">boAt Airdopes 212</a> adds the first for Rs 300 less. For all-day comfort and small ears, the Redmi is still the one we would hand over.</p>

=== redmi-buds-8-active
<p>The Buds 8 Active is an open-style pair, without silicone tips, around a large 14.2mm driver. Open buds need that size to give any bass at all, and here it works at moderate volume. You keep hearing traffic and announcements.</p>
<p>It supports two devices, has IP54 and weighs 3.8 grams per bud in a very small 28 gram case. Seven hours and 37 in total is average.</p>
<p>At Rs 4,499 the question is whether you want an open fit. If you do, it is a good-value example. If you are only looking for the best sound or quiet, a sealed pair such as the <a href="/products/anker-soundcore-r50i-nc">Soundcore R50i NC</a> is the better use of Rs 500 more.</p>
