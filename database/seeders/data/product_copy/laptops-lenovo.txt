<p>The V15 G4 with a Core i3-1315U, 8GB and 512GB is the sort of laptop that sits on thousands of reception desks. At Rs 84,000 it has sensible storage, a number pad, a LAN port on most units, and a removable base for upgrades.</p>

=== lenovo-v15-g6-2025
<p>The V15 G6 costs Rs 62,999 with Intel's N100, 8GB and 256GB. The N100 is a small four-core chip made for low power. It is fine for documents, web pages, YouTube and a shop's billing software, and uses so little electricity that a small UPS keeps it alive for long.</p>
<p>It is a good replacement for an ageing desktop at a counter. For a student it is borderline, because video calls with screen sharing push it hard.</p>
