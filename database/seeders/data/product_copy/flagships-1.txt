=== iphone-18-pro
<p>The iPhone 18 Pro is the flagship for people who do not want a giant phone. At 6.3 inches and 211 grams it carries everything Apple puts in the Pro Max, the same chip, cameras and charging, in a body that fits a hand and a pocket. It went on sale in Nepal on 29 September 2026.</p>

<p>The body is a single piece of aluminium with a Ceramic Shield back and front, in Black, Silver, Glacier and Burgundy. <h3>A20 Pro and memory</h3>
<p>The A20 Pro is Apple's first 2nm chip, with a six-core processor, a seven-core graphics unit and two Neural Engines, together with 12GB of memory. Everyday use does not need it. <h3>Cameras, and what variable aperture really does</h3>
<p>All three rear cameras use 48MP sensors. The main one has an aperture that physically opens and closes between f/1.48, f/1.8, f/2.8 and f/4.0. In dim light it opens wide. For a group of eight people, a plate of food or a document, you can close it so that the nearest and farthest parts are both sharp, which phone cameras have struggled with for years. It is also a creative control in video.</p>
<p>The telephoto is 4x, equal to a 100mm lens, and a crop from the middle of its sensor gives 8x at what Apple calls optical quality. The ultra wide covers 120 degrees and doubles as a macro camera. On the front, the 18MP Center Stage camera has a square sensor, so wide group selfies work with the phone upright.</p>
<p>Video remains the reason many professionals choose iPhone: 4K at up to 120 frames in Dolby Vision, ProRes and ProRes RAW with an external drive, and Log for colour grading.</p>
<h3>Battery and charging</h3>
<p>Apple quotes up to 34 hours of video playback for the version with a SIM slot, the one normally sold in our region. The eSIM-only American model is rated at 36.
